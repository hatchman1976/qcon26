## Speaker notes

The buckets represent accumulating verification and repair work. The Lemming represents the expertise and response capacity needed to protect performance. Cheap generation can move checking, maintenance and recovery work elsewhere, consuming attention and capacity even as visible output increases.

The dotted line warns about the remaining buffer. Crossing it begins consuming protected error margin, while the solid line marks the actual functional limit. A warning helps only if people have practical understanding, time, authority and means to reduce demand, challenge output, stop further action or recover.

The reference marks the scenario’s starting state. This is an illustrative possible journey, not a measured trajectory or claim that all AI output is workslop. Organisational choices and usable feedback shape whether people can detect and respond to a convincing mistake in their actual work.
