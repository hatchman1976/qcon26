## Speaker notes

The buckets represent accumulating verification and repair work. The Lemming represents the protective capacity needed to understand the work and keep it within acceptable performance. Accumulated work can obscure and overwhelm that capacity even while visible output increases.

The reference marks the scenario's starting state. This is an illustrative possible journey, not a measured whole-system trajectory or a prediction that AI inevitably causes failure.

The dotted line is a warning about the remaining buffer. Crossing it begins consuming the protected error margin; it does not itself establish unacceptable performance. The solid line represents the actual functional limit. Knowing that a warning has fired does not supply the understanding, authority, time or capacity needed to respond.

Organisational choices, expertise, usable feedback and the ability to change or stop the work determine how these pressures are managed.
