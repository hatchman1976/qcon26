## Speaker notes

Start with just the red operating point: the whole socio-technical system's current state. On the next click the AI ball rolls in from the left and settles over it. The artwork is a visual shorthand for AI-mediated work at the same operating state. It is not a second operating point, a person, or a fourth constraint. Ask what changes in cost, effort, evidence and recovery. The following step expands the view to the same three boundaries and the performance-side error margin. Reduced-motion mode shows the settled ball without rolling; the reveal still requires the presenter to advance.

**AI in the feedback and action channels**

AI is not automatically a new controller. It influences channels used by existing controllers. On the feedback side it can gather and summarize observations, find contradictions and support testing; fluent summaries can also conceal uncertainty. On the action side it can present options, draft decisions and translate intent into output; plausible detail can exceed the communicator’s intended criteria.

AI output changes the operating state when people or automation admit and enact the resulting decisions, not merely when output is generated. The risk is conditional: AI can strengthen testing, diagnosis, challenge and verification. Risk rises when generation and action become easier faster than assurance and correction.

Rasmussen (1997) describes behaviour and work activity migrating toward the boundary. Cook and Rasmussen (2005) explicitly use operating-point terminology. The ball remains shorthand for one current whole-system state.
