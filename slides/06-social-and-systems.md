## Speaker notes

The system is neither the people alone nor the technology alone. Their interaction sits inside a social control hierarchy running from workers and staff through management and the company to regulators and government.

The hierarchy has two nested scales. Society governs and regulates organizations; organizations distribute authority through the company, management, staff, and workers. The company sits at the seam: controlled by the society around it while acting as a controller of the work inside it.

Different research disciplines observe different levels, while environmental stressors act across them. Information has to travel upward while decisions and constraints travel downward, and each layer adapts on a different timescale. The final reveal locates the audience and their organization inside this system rather than outside it: control is distributed, not owned by any single actor.
