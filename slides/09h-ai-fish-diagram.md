## Speaker notes

Open the camera from the operating point onto the familiar model. The display deliberately contains only the three functional boundary lines, the performance-side dotted error margin and the AI ball. There is no fourth AI boundary. After the camera settles, the ball wanders from the middle, with a bias toward the performance side, as in the original diagram. The trajectory is illustrative, not measured, and no geometric center is an ideal state. Organizational choices and usable feedback shape the actual trajectory. Reduced-motion mode keeps the ball stationary. The next three steps inspect economics, workload and the error margin. They are followed by the existing human-behaviour evidence and control slides.
