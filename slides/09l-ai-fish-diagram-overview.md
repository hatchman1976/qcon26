## Speaker notes

Bring the pressures back together. Cheap generation can add checking and repair work elsewhere. When that work accumulates faster than effective verification capacity, it can consume the attention, expertise and response capacity that protect performance.

A warning is useful only if someone can interpret it and act while there is still room to recover. Preserve time, authority and practical means to reduce demand, challenge the output, stop further action or restore service.

The preceding scenario is illustrative. It does not establish that all AI output is workslop or that pressure always produces the same path. The practical question is whether the organisation can detect and respond to a convincing mistake under the conditions of its actual work.
