Resilience Engineering When AI Moves the Operating Point

## Visual treatment

Show a Blocker Lemming with its arms outstretched. Its head turns left, then right, then back again in a continuous loop. Keep a static pose when reduced motion is enabled.

The speaker name uses 58px Archivo Black, centered at 608px from the top of the slide, 40px lower than its previous position.

## Speaker notes

Open with the audience promise: this is not a replay of the 2024 talk.

In 2024, I used Rasmussen's model to explain the geometry of safety: boundaries, margins, and continuous drift.

In 2026, the question is whether the organization's feedback loops can keep pace when AI accelerates the production of candidate change while expert attention remains scarce.
