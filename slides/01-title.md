## Speaker notes

Open with the audience promise: this is not a replay of the 2024 talk.

In 2024, I used Rasmussen's model to explain the geometry of safety: boundaries, margins, and continuous drift.

In 2026, the question is whether the organization's feedback loops can keep pace when AI accelerates the production of candidate change while expert attention remains scarce.
