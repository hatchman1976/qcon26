## Slide copy and sequence

**Socio Technical Systems**

...in organizational development (Socio Technical Systems) is an approach to complex organizational work design that recognizes the interaction between people and technology in workplaces.

The definition uses bold 36px Comic Neue.

## Speaker notes


A socio-technical system cannot be understood by examining either people or technology in isolation.

The visual contrasts studying people and technology separately with seeing people as part of the system. Its connections emphasise the interactions between them.

**People are part of control**

People and organisations are adaptive components and controllers within the socio-technical system, not an external human input. Locally sensible adaptations can preserve control, or interact to create conditions for failure. Rasmussen connects effective control with usable objectives, feedback, competence, coordination and commitment.
