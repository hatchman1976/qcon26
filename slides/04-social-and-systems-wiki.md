# Slide 4 — Socio-Technical Systems Definition

## Slide copy

**Socio Technical Systems**

> ...in organizational development (Socio Technical Systems) is an approach to complex organizational work design that recognizes the interaction between people and technology in workplaces.

## Speaker notes

Establish the working definition used in the 2024 talk: a socio-technical system cannot be understood by examining either people or technology in isolation.

1. Show the people and complex system separately.
2. Frame both with magnifying glasses to emphasize separate inspection.
3. Show people distributed across the complex system.
4. Keep bright orange points with yellow highlights and a bold rim pulsing at random arrow locations to highlight interactions between nodes.

This names the system the talk will examine and prepares Rasmussen's control hierarchy later in the sequence.
