## Speaker notes

The heading uses 64px Archivo Black to fit on one line. The definition uses 36px Comic Neue with 1.2 line spacing, positioned above the illustrations.

Establish the working definition used in the 2024 talk: a socio-technical system cannot be understood by examining either people or technology in isolation.

The visual contrasts studying people and technology separately with seeing people on the system's nodes.

Reveal 1: show the people and complex system separately. Reveal 2: frame both with magnifying glasses to emphasize separate inspection. Reveal 3: show people distributed across the complex system. Reveal 4: keep bright orange points with yellow highlights and a bold rim pulsing at random arrow locations to draw attention to interactions between nodes.

This names the system the talk will examine and prepares Rasmussen's control hierarchy later in the sequence.

**People are part of control**

People and organisations are adaptive components and controllers within the socio-technical system, not an external human input. Locally sensible adaptations can preserve control, or interact to create conditions for failure. Rasmussen connects effective control with usable objectives, feedback, competence, coordination and commitment.
