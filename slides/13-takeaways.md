# Slide 13 — Takeaways

## Slide copy

Three things to remember

1. **Unit cost is not total cost.** Cheaper tokens can coexist with higher aggregate spend.
2. **The point and the envelope can both move.** Usage changes operating state; economics and capacity can reshape constraints.
3. **Do not protect throughput by consuming assurance.** Govern demand, preserve verification, and retain recovery.

## Speaker notes

State the distinction directly: 2024 was about seeing the geometry of drift; 2026 is about whether organizational control loops can keep pace with AI-amplified change.

Complete the synthesis:

“AI can make each action cheaper while making the aggregate system more expensive. Every admitted AI output carries two costs: generation and assurance. If throughput is protected, safety margin pays the difference.”

Repeat the visual grammar: usage moves the operating point; a real change in sustainable financial capacity moves the outer economic boundary; budgets and ROI targets move an inner guardrail. The distinction is what makes the dynamic-envelope argument testable rather than metaphorical.
