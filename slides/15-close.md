## Speaker notes

Resolve the distinction in the final spoken lines:

“In 2024, I asked whether we could see the boundaries. In 2026, AI can move the operating point faster than our organization can perceive, verify, and correct it. AI can make each action cheaper while making the aggregate system more expensive. Every admitted AI output carries two costs: generation and assurance. If throughput is protected, safety margin pays the difference.”

Leave the operating point moving within a controlled regime—not frozen at the center. Show sufficient margin, small excursions, and responsive feedback.
