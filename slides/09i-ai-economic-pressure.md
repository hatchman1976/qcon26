## Speaker notes

ETR surveyed 1,636 technology leaders, June 2–July 5, 2026. Its 47% is reported spending above plan, not a prevalence estimate of annual budgets exhausted early. Among respondents reallocating to cover overruns, 61% cut contractor/consultant spending, 49% legacy infrastructure modernization or hardware refresh, 36% non-AI licenses and 28% internal headcount budgets. The slide's 49% applies only to organizations reallocating budgets to cover AI overruns, not all respondents. These are reported budget choices, not a measurement of subsequent reliability harm. The Uber case is an executive's reported account rather than audited finances.

Meta's October 2025 Hyperion announcement describes a joint venture with Blue Owl funds to finance roughly $27 billion of data-center development. This brings in outside capital rather than cutting another internal budget, with future lease and guarantee obligations. The amount is project development cost, not Meta's annual AI bill. The portfolio-return example is an illustrative management choice. The three rows distinguish reduced infrastructure investment, external financing and product reprioritization.

Cost control can preserve other margins by dropping low-value work. It transfers pressure when required output stays constant but relevant expertise, maintenance or assurance disappears. More AI expenditure moves the operating state; budgets remain targets, not an extra dotted economic boundary.

**Whole-workflow economics**

Include model use, platform commitments, integration, tools and data, evaluation, human verification, rework and recovery in outcome cost. Longer contexts, retries, agent loops and additional workflows can consume unit-cost savings. This is conditional, not a prediction that cheaper AI must increase spending. Evaluate work transferred or deferred to reviewers, maintainers and responders, along with future coupling, congestion and recovery costs.

Preserve capacity by reducing low-value demand, bounding loops, changing scope and routing or caching where verified quality permits. Organizations may also redirect portfolios toward products with stronger expected returns, as the visible example suggests. Keep verification and recovery funded. AI can improve tests and diagnosis as well as generation. If required output stays constant while relevant expertise or review is removed, workload can rise and performance margin can shrink.

Rasmussen supplies the adaptation/control framework; Chapter 5 supplies a contemporary resilience synthesis. The software economics and response examples are this talk’s application, not AI findings from the 1997 paper.
