## Speaker notes

The system is neither the people alone nor the technology alone. Their interaction sits inside a hierarchy running from workers and staff through management, the company, regulators, and government.

Different research disciplines observe different levels, while environmental stressors act across the hierarchy. The final reveal locates the audience and their organization inside this system rather than outside it.
