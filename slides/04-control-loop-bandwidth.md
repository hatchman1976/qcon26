# Slide 04 — Control-loop timing

## Slide copy

2024 foregrounded the geometry

**2026 foregrounds control-loop timing.**

2024: Where is the operating point drifting?<br>
2026: Can sensing, verification, and correction keep pace?

**Rate, gain, and delay change the outcome.**

## Speaker notes

The three classes of constraint remain: economic viability, acceptable workload, and acceptable performance.

What has changed is the tempo. AI can increase the arrival rate and variability of candidate changes. At the same time, layoffs can reduce expert verification capacity and remove contextual knowledge.

The actual operating point can therefore move faster than the organization's perception of it.

Cloud can remain a short verbal precedent: abstraction increased leverage before operational guardrails caught up. It should not remain the narrative hinge, because the distinctive 2026 argument is the asymmetry between generation and verification.
