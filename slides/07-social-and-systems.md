## Speaker notes

Reveal 1 introduces the social control hierarchy surrounding work, from workers and staff through management and the company to regulators and government.

Reveal 2 adds the research disciplines that observe different levels and the environmental stressors that act across them. Information has to travel upward while decisions and constraints travel downward, and each layer adapts on a different timescale. Control is distributed rather than owned by any single actor.
