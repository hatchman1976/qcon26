## Speaker notes

The previous slide shows people working within a complex system. This slide introduces the social control hierarchy surrounding that work.

Reveal 1: introduce Rasmussen's social control hierarchy. Reveal 2: add the research disciplines and environmental stressors that cross its levels. Control is distributed rather than owned by any single actor, and information must travel upward while decisions and constraints travel downward. Each layer adapts on a different timescale.

Source slide and hierarchy artwork: https://srecon24.hatchman76.com/#/social-and-systems

**Goals, evidence and authority**

The control structure is the distributed network of decision makers, goals, constraints, information, competence and authority around the work. Observations and outcomes inform decisions; decisions and constraints change the work. Feedback must reach actors with the resources and authority to respond.

When AI enters these channels, re-examine problem framing, attention, verification, skill retention under pressure, communicated intent and feedback fidelity across the hierarchy. Task-level studies do not establish the accumulated effects on a whole socio-technical system.
