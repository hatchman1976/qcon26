# Slide 09 — Multiple closing margins

## Slide copy

When economic room narrows

**One operating state. Multiple closing margins.**

1. AI scale can raise model spend and candidate-change demand.
2. A financial shock can contract the envelope; a cost target can tighten its guardrail.
3. Cost cuts can restore that margin by reducing human capacity.
4. If throughput is fixed, assurance becomes the pressure-release valve.

Diagram labels:

- Economic margin
- Actual economic boundary
- Budget / ROI guardrail
- Workload margin
- Performance margin
- Actual operating point
- Organization's lagging estimate
- Workload–performance joint-constraint region

**Every admitted AI output carries two costs: generation and assurance.**

## Speaker notes

Animate one actual operating point, not one point per boundary. More AI use, infrastructure consumption, and admitted work move the point. A genuine change in funding, revenue, runway, financing, or sustainable cost can reshape the actual economic boundary. A budget or ROI target moves the inner guardrail. Never use one event to move all three.

Then branch the response. A safe economic controller can reduce low-value demand through admission control, rate limits, model routing, caching, batching, or scope reduction. A risk-transferring response protects output by cutting people or assurance capacity.

In the second branch, reduced expert capacity and rising review demand move the same operating state toward the workload boundary. Overtime, context switching, and attention rationing can preserve visible delivery while consuming adaptive capacity.

If scope or work in progress is reduced, the point can return to a controlled regime. If economic pressure holds throughput constant, review, testing, maintenance, and weak-signal investigation may be shed. The same point can then move through the region near the workload boundary toward the workload–performance joint-constraint region.

Do not imply a precisely known three-boundary intersection. Show multiple margins closing and name only the joint-constraint region that the evidence supports.

If the visual includes the organization's estimate, render it as a trailing shadow of the actual point and label it explicitly. It represents delayed perception, not a second operating state.

Avoid implying that exhausted people care less. Their discretionary adaptive capacity has been exhausted, so attention is rationed to immediate survival.

Keep the causal claim conditional. AI and layoffs do not mechanically cause performance failure; the risk-transferring adaptation occurs when admitted demand exceeds verification capacity and the organization refuses to pay in scope or latency.
