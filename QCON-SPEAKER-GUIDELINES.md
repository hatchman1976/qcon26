**You will be presenting from your laptop.** QCon does NOT provide a presentation laptop. Please bring all necessary plugs and adapters. Additionally, **please ensure your laptop has AT LEAST a 50% charge on your battery.** All laptops should be plugged into the power outlet that will be at the podium in your session room.

Remember that your presentation should be 50 minutes long — you can also have a 40 minute presentation with a 10 minute Q&A session if you'd like. **Please do not plan a talk that is shorter than 40 minutes as this might affect the attendee experience.**

## Overview

Slides are important for our QCon audience. If you are able to share your slides, we highly encourage you to do this. This is especially important for our Video-Only Pass holders.

We upload the session and keynote slides and make them available on the QCon websites. **Please email a .pdf copy of your slides to [speaking@qconsf.com](mailto:speaking@qconsf.com) following your presentation.** If you noted in your speaker registration form that you opted-out of sharing, a no-slide icon has been added to your presentation online.

Additionally, prior to giving your presentation, **your slides should be reviewed. Please send us a copy of your slides by November 13th. These do not have to be your final slides** — we understand sometimes things change up until your presentation time. This review is meant to catch anything that might not resonate with our audience. You should also ensure you share these with your Track Host.

## Presentation Preparation

As you are finalizing your slides, please ensure you refrain from making statements regarding politics, wars, military conflicts, etc. QCon is dedicated to a harassment-free conference experience for everyone. We ask you to please read and respect our code of conduct: [https://qconferences.com/code-conduct/](https://qconferences.com/code-conduct/). If you have any concerns about a slide or content that you want to present and that you feel might be in the gray area of our code of conduct (i.e. a political statement, an image you want to share, etc.), please bring them to our attention before the conference, so we can make a joint decision and avoid an uncomfortable experience during your presentation.

**Slides should be in a 16:9 ratio format. There is no slide template, but you are welcome to use the QCon logo.**

## ✅ DO the Following with Your Slides

### Design Related

- Presentation should be widescreen — 16:9 ratio.
- **Use a font size of 32 or more**, be conscious of the amount of code and text used. Someone sitting in the back of the room should be able to read your slides (the back of the room is further than you think).
- Use Sans Serif fonts — these tend to be the best fonts for presentations.
- Use dark font on a light background — this is the best way to be accessible to the entire audience.
- Limit the number of slides — a good rule of thumb is one slide per minute.
- Use good quality images — if it reinforces your presentation, then make the image the only item on the slide (be sure you have the appropriate rights to use images as well).
- If you are using code — split it up on different slides to highlight the code you are trying to describe. Doing this ensures you don't cram small text on one slide and allows you to have a good offline version of the code you want to demo.

### Try the 5/5/5 Rule (or Any Combination)

Don't overwhelm the audience with a lot of words on your slides. Instead, try and simplify your slides keeping them short.

- 5 words per line of text.
- 5 lines of text per slide.
- No more than 5 text heavy slides in a row.

## ❌ DON'T Do the Following with Your Slides

### Design Related

- Use too many special effects — these can distract from your message.
- Have a lot of bullet points or words on one slide.
- **Use a company logo on every slide — keep company logos to the first 2–3 slides and the last 2–3 slides only.**
- Use font smaller than 32 — put yourself in the attendee's seat.
- Use images to fill space — unless they add value to your presentation, don't use them. Test your images if you do use some to ensure they are not pixelated when projected on a larger screen.

### Presenting Your Slides

- Don't take a long time to explain what your talk is about.
- Don't read directly from your slides — include keywords, takeaways, and talking points to engage with the audience more.
- Don't make product presentations or pitches, that is against QCon values. If you'd like to include your company logo, please do so in the first few slides or the last few slides so that the attendees do not think it's a sponsored session.

**Important note:** In all session rooms where there are confidence monitors — you will have access to **SLIDES only**, the confidence monitors **will not show speaker notes**. Please plan accordingly. Your laptop, however, will be on a table or podium near you where you can have your notes. They will not be available on the confidence monitors.

**Do NOT let QCon be the first time you are running through slides. We recommend testing your presentation (and try breaking it) so you can be prepared for any situation.**
